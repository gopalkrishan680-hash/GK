Your voice sample
Upload a clear recording of your own voice (10–30 s is best; mp3, wav, m4a, a WhatsApp voice note or even a video). Speak it the way you narrate your videos, with feeling and at your normal pace: the clone copies the style of the sample, so a flat, rushed reading gives a flat, rushed voice.

  1. Press Copy next prompt and paste it into ChatGPT or Gemini.
  2. When the image is ready, open it in ChatGPT/Gemini and tap Share → Stickman Studio. It goes straight into the selected (yellow) card. You can also copy it and tap Paste image.
  3. When the image is ready, copy it (long-press the image → Copy). Tap the card it belongs to (it turns yellow), then tap Paste image. If your phone cannot copy the image, save it and use Choose file….
  4. When the image is ready, copy it (right-click → Copy image). Click the card it belongs to (it turns yellow), then press Ctrl+V. The yellow box at the bottom right always shows where the paste will go.
  5. The app cuts it into its 9 scenes and selects the next empty card for you. Repeat until all are done.

Quality tip: each scene gets only a ninth of the image. Gemini can make 4K images (about 1280×720 per scene, which is sharp). ChatGPT makes at most 1536×1024 (about 500 px per scene), so the app sharpens and upscales those panels for you.
