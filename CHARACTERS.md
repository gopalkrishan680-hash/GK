# Character Bible – Animated Short Films

Ye characters is channel ki **har** silhouette-style video mein same rahenge.
Naam, look aur rishte kabhi change nahi honge. Nayi kahani mein naya character
sirf tab aayega jab story ko uski zaroorat ho, aur wo bhi yahan add hoga.

## Main character

### Rohan (हीरो)
- **Naam:** Rohan Kumar
- **Look:** Kaala silhouette, gol sar, **koi baal nahi** (plain head), safed oval aankhein, bhauhein expression ke hisaab se.
- **Glow (rim):** Neutral halka cream `(205, 195, 180)`. Emotion ke hisaab se rang badalta hai:
  - Jalan / gussa → laal `(245, 60, 45)`
  - Darr / tension → neela-violet `(120, 140, 255)`
  - Khushi / sukoon → sunehra `(250, 205, 130)`
- **Personality:** Aam middle-class ladka, sensitive, apni feelings andar rakhta hai, end mein seekhta hai.
- **Umar:** Kahani ke hisaab se badal sakti hai (school wali kahaniyon mein 10th class, office wali kahaniyon mein young professional), par naam aur look wahi rahega.

## Family

### Maa (माँ)
- **Look:** Peeche **joda (bun)**, kandhe par **pallu**.
- **Glow:** Garam sunehra `(245, 205, 140)`.
- **Personality:** Hamesha khana-pina poochti hai, chupchaap fikr karti hai.

### Papa
- **Look:** **Chashma (glasses)**, upar flat baal, thoda bada body (scale 1.08).
- **Glow:** Halka cream `(210, 200, 185)` → emotional pal mein sunehra.
- **Personality:** Bahar se strict dikhte hain, andar se bahut pyaar karte hain.

## Dost

### Aman
- **Look:** **Spiky baal** (5 nukile spikes).
- **Glow:** Garam peela `(222, 205, 170)`. Udaasi mein neela `(150, 170, 215)`.
- **Rishta:** Rohan ka best friend. Bahar se "perfect life", andar se apni pareshaniyan.

## Side characters (zaroorat padne par)
- **Boss:** Chashma + flat baal, scale 1.06 (sirf office wali kahaniyon mein).
- **"Sharma ji ka beta":** Sirf zikr hota hai, screen par nahi aata.

## Video style (har video mein same)
- Kaale silhouette characters + glow outline, background blur (depth of field).
- Side mein emotion meter (thermometer jaisa), label Hindi mein (jaise "जलन", "डर").
- Captions: Hindi (Devanagari) + jo shabd English mein natural lagte hain wo English mein.
- Caption font: Baloo 2 (bold), narration font: Martel.
- Shuru aur end mein kaali screen par ek line, end mein bada title.
- Vertical (9:16) video mein text bada: captions ~64px, title ~190px (1080×1920 par).

## Thumbnail style (TEMPER jaisa – har video ke liye same)
Thumbnail **video ka frame nahi** hoga. Har baar alag se ek "poster" banega:
- **Background:** Poora kaala (lagbhag `(6, 6, 8)`), koi kamra ya scene nahi.
- **Glow:** Har character ke peeche uske emotion ke rang ka bada, soft glow (Rohan ka emotion rang, Papa/Maa ka sunehra, wagairah).
- **Characters:** Bade, poster jaise khade (poora body ya kamar tak), outline glow tez. 1-2 characters, ek bada aur ek chhota/side mein.
- **Pose:** Simple aur saaf – kamar par haath, haath mein koi cheez (marksheet, phone, trophy) jo kahani batati hai.
- **Title:** Upar ek ya do shabd, **Montserrat Black**, safed, bada (9:16 mein ~250px, 16:9 mein ~150px), halki kaali shadow. Zaroorat ho to ek shabd/number emotion ke rang mein.
- **Extra text:** Kam se kam. Kahani wali cheez (jaise "58%") prop ke andar likhi ho, alag caption nahi.
- **Size:** 16:9 video → 1280×720, 9:16 Shorts → 1080×1920.
